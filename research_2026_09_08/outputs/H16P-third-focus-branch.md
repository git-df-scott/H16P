# Higher-order focus branch: coexistence follow-up

**Search-direction correction:** Published focus nonexistence/uniqueness results obstruct the proposed exact-focus precursor. The numerical record below is retained, but its suggested continuation is superseded by [the correction](H16P-focus-route-correction.md).

No five-cycle candidate was found. The remote-cycle evidence persists along much of the tested higher-order focus branch, but the additional outer cycle around that focus is still missing.

The bounded continuation used b=1/6,1/3,1/2,2/3 and k=1/20,1/10,1/5,2/5. At each shape, the code searched sign-changing fifth-order coefficient brackets in a between −2.7 and −0.3, on the same cubic-degeneracy branch used previously. It selected a numerical root with a nonzero seventh coefficient nearest the preceding selected root. This is a bounded branch selection, not an exhaustive classification of all degeneracies.

Thirteen shapes yielded selected candidates. Three yielded only numerically near-zero seventh coefficients in the searched brackets and received no finite-cycle profile. For the selected points, the largest absolute cubic or quintic return coefficient was 1.53e−12; seventh coefficients ranged from about 0.00936 to 0.06219. Rational approximations, floating integration, and root-finding are not an exact simultaneous-degeneracy certificate.

Each selected field received 49 upper and 33 lower section samples, producing **2,132 complete half-passages**, with no unresolved return. Eleven fields had one sampled remote crossing. Two had none. None had an upper outer crossing in the sampled radius range 0.03 to 10^7.

All eleven remote brackets retained their endpoint signs in **44 tighter half-passages**; the largest change was 3.01e−13. These are numerical brackets, not interval-certified isolated cycles.

The study therefore supports the remote-cycle component of this construction over several shapes, while failing to supply its extra outer-cycle component. It does not exclude cycles between samples, beyond the sampled range, on another degeneracy branch, or away from the tested shape range. A generic unfolding producing three small cycles has also not been verified.

Another 22,000 characters of the inherited fold-continuation log were read during this work. The coverage ledger remains explicit that raw repository reading is unfinished; inherited unresolved outer returns have not been converted into cycle exclusions.
