# Finite Q4 continuation

No counterexample found. Thirty-five tested finite fields retained three sampled return-map crossings around the original focus. A thirty-sixth fit approached a reversible center locus and is not counted as three isolated cycles.

The fields use the original Q4 base family with perturbations
\[
\delta P=\tau x+(2u+w)xy,\qquad
\delta Q=\tau y+u(x^2-y^2)+vxy.
\]
At moduli r=1/2,1,2, I fixed v=−epsilon and fitted the remaining three controls to equal forward/backward half-passages at three fixed section radii. These radii correspond to unperturbed energy fractions 1/4,1/2,3/4. Both signs of epsilon were followed through magnitudes 0.001,0.01,0.03,0.1,0.2,0.4.

This uses the actual perturbed ODE rather than a first-order Melnikov approximation. The fitting Jacobian comes from variational equations, including the moving event-time correction. An independent finite-difference check agreed to 3.36e−12.

The fit and profile calculations used 6,102 half-passages. All profile returns resolved. A further 72 tighter half-passages retained every bracket endpoint sign at six selected continuation endpoints, with maximum change 1.70e−14. Ten additional half-passages checked the Jacobian. These are nonvalidated calculations, not certified periodic orbits or exhaustive root counts.

At r=1/2, epsilon=−0.4, the fit approached
\[
P=-y-\tfrac94x^2+y^2,\qquad Q=x-\tfrac{27}{20}xy.
\]
This exact projection is reversible under (x,y)→(−x,y) with time reversal. Its equilibria (0,0) and (0,1) have zero trace and positive determinants 1 and 7/20, hence are reversible centers. The numerical fitted field is only near this locus; its nearly vanishing sampled displacement does not certify isolated cycles.

Numerical equilibrium enumeration found a second focus in several other negative-perturbation fields. Their current return profiles test the original-focus nest, so they do not establish a whole-plane count. The next meaningful check is a complete return section around that second focus, while retaining the same coefficient vector and the three original-focus brackets.
