# Historical five-or-more cycle claims: source and status ledger

Audit date: **2026-09-04**. Object: one autonomous smooth real planar polynomial vector field of total degree at most two. A limit cycle must be an isolated periodic orbit. This ledger separates construction claims, upper-bound claims, and different mathematical objects. “Published” records editorial status; it does not establish correctness.

**Result:** no accepted five-cycle example was located. Four remains the accepted lower bound in the primary literature checked. A 2026 journal paper genuinely claims six, but its simultaneous count fails a direct coefficient check. An August 2026 author-posted abstract claims seven; its full manuscript and asserted example were unavailable. Neither changes the record established by reproducible examples.

The most recent expert primary status statement found is Artés–Cairó–Llibre, *Phase Portraits of the Families V to X*, **QTDS 25:145, published 2026-08-07**, Introduction §1: maximum unknown, examples reaching four, four still conjectural. Gasull–Santana, **Proc. AMS 153(2) (2025), 669–677**, Introduction, also explicitly records unknown uniform finiteness of H(2). These support a status assessment, not a proof that later or inaccessible claims cannot succeed. [August 2026 article](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s12346-026-01563-4), [2025 accepted postprint](https://ddd.uab.cat/pub/artpub/2025/309367/GasSan24-Postprint.pdf).

## Shi's first five-cycle claim: author-corrected before the four-cycle publication

**Songling Shi, 1978 first preprint: H(2)>=5 — CORRECTED BY THE AUTHOR.** Shi's own retrospective, *Hilbert's Sixteenth Problem (the second part): Its Present State*, **Ann. Sci. Math. Québec 14(2) (1990), 193–206**, pp.202–203, describes combining two Sommerfeld-type cycles with three Bautin small cycles. He dates his five-cycle lecture to **1978-12-18**. On p.203 he explains that correcting the sign of Bautin's **third Poincaré–Lyapunov constant V3** destroyed one cycle: conditions (*) were unrealizable, leaving four. This is direct author correction, not a later conjectural rejection. The original five-cycle preprint itself was not retrieved. [Primary retrospective, pp.201–203](https://www.labmath.uqam.ca/~annales/volumes/14-2/PDF/193-206.pdf).

The attempted base family was

\[
\dot x=-y+\ell x^2+5axy+ny^2,\qquad
\dot y=x+ax^2+(3\ell+5n)xy,\qquad an\ne0.
\]

Writing \(m=5a\), \(b=3\ell+5n\), p.202 gives

\[
V_3=a(2a^2+2n^2+\ell n)
\big[(b+2\ell)(\ell+n)(b+n)-am(2\ell+b+n)\big].
\]

The proposed conditions (*) are

\[
\begin{aligned}
3a^2-\ell(\ell+2n)&<0,\\
25a^2+12n(\ell+2n)&<0,\\
\ell(3\ell+5n)^2-5a^2(3\ell+5n)+na^2&<0,\\
a^2(5\ell+8n)^2-
\big[(2\ell+5n)^2+15a^2\big]
\big[25a^2+3n(2\ell+5n)\big]&>0,\\
V_3&<0.
\end{aligned}
\tag{*}
\]

Shi reports that these corrected conditions are infeasible, so they do not supply a five-cycle coefficient tuple. The retrospective cites Shi's *On Limit Cycles of Plane Quadratic Systems*, **Scientia Sinica 24 (1981), 153–159**, reference [13], for the correction. OCR renders V3 ambiguously; the original p.203 scan unambiguously shows subscript **3**, not 5.

The [earlier repository README](https://github.com/git-df-scott/H16P/blob/5bcfe1172c124cb9a162a2e88c26aa5be26b40c1/README.md#L80) calls this the fifth focus quantity; the source scan says V3. That historical README remains intact at the linked source commit, and this audit uses the source notation. Modern interval arithmetic could test a surviving explicit field, but the reported infeasible conditions provide none.

## Explicit coefficients and independent check of the six-cycle argument

The 2022 preprint gives

\[
\dot x=\lambda x-y-(7+\varepsilon_1)x^2+(5+\varepsilon_2)xy+3y^2,
\qquad \dot y=x+x^2-6xy.
\]

At \(\varepsilon_1=\varepsilon_2=0\), it studies a third-order weak focus at the origin with \(\lambda=0\), then at \((0,1/3)\) with \(\lambda=-5/3\), and adds the counts. The published 2026 family generalizes this to

\[
\dot x=\lambda x-y-\frac73n x^2+5axy+ny^2,
\qquad \dot y=x+ax^2-2nxy,\quad n\ne0.
\]

**The following is this audit's algebra, not a claim of an external published rebuttal.** At \(E_0=(0,0)\) and \(E_1=(0,1/n)\),

\[
J(E_0)=\begin{pmatrix}\lambda&-1\\1&0\end{pmatrix},\qquad
J(E_1)=\begin{pmatrix}\lambda+5a/n&1\\-1&0\end{pmatrix}.
\]

Consequently the two trace-zero conditions are \(\lambda=0\) and \(\lambda=-5a/n\). They concern different vector fields unless \(a=0\). At \(a=0,\lambda=0\), the system is reversible under \((x,y,t)\mapsto(-x,y,-t)\); its elementary monodromic equilibria on the symmetry axis are centers, rather than simultaneous order-three weak foci. The paper's third-focus expression itself vanishes when \(a=0\).

There is also a separate inference problem: an order-three weak focus has a local cyclicity statement under a suitable unfolding. It does not already contain three isolated periodic orbits at the degenerate parameter. A common coefficient choice and return-map roots are needed to establish simultaneously existing cycles. Neither source supplies them.

For reproduction at \(a=1,n=3\), the two exact candidate base fields have \(\lambda=0\) and \(\lambda=-5/3\). Other equilibria, if any, have

\[
y=(1+ax)/(2n),\qquad
(33a^2-28n^2)x^2+(12n\lambda+30a)x-3=0.
\]

At either base field this quadratic's discriminant is \(-1728\), so there are exactly two real equilibria. The second focus at \(\lambda=0\) has eigenvalues \((5\pm i\sqrt{11})/6\); the origin at \(\lambda=-5/3\) has \((-5\pm i\sqrt{11})/6\). These confirm directly that one focus is strong where the other is weak. The explicit family can be numerically tested, but it is not a verified six-cycle seed.

## A rigorous distribution restriction

Zegeling's **Theorem 1.2, printed p.3, proof §7**, *Nests of limit cycles in quadratic systems*, **Advances in Nonlinear Analysis 13 (2024), 20240012**, proves that distributions have the form \((n,0)\) or \((n,1)\). The article fills essential omissions in Zhang's 2002 argument. Its introduction also records that each quadratic limit cycle surrounds exactly one focus, and at most two nests can occur. Thus a five-cycle construction can have distribution **(5,0) or (4,1)**; **(3,2), (3,3), (2,2), and three nests are excluded**. This is an additional obstruction to the asserted six-cycle 3:3 architecture. It does not bound the larger nest by three. [Primary full PDF](https://d-nb.info/1332906729/34), DOI [10.1515/anona-2024-0012](https://doi.org/10.1515/anona-2024-0012).

## Coverage note: the 2024–25 criticism of Ilyashenko's finiteness proof

**Melvin Yeung**, arXiv:2402.12506v1 (2024-02-19), is now published under the title **Dulac's Theorem Revisited**, **Qualitative Theory of Dynamical Systems 24:57 (2025)**, accepted 2025-01-02 and published **2025-01-27**, DOI **10.1007/s12346-025-01220-2**. Thus this is published technical criticism, not merely an unreviewed 2024 preprint. [Publisher and dates](https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s12346-025-01220-2), [institutional publication record](https://documentserver.uhasselt.be/handle/1942/45363), [full preprint](https://arxiv.org/html/2402.12506v1).

The preprint's Statement 2.1 and Section 6 exhibit failure of a closure assertion used on p.198 of Ilyashenko's 1991 monograph to obtain leading asymptotic terms. Its example glues transit maps of an alternant polycycle; it does **not** construct a quadratic field with infinitely many or five limit cycles. The article preserves the established hyperbolic-polycycle case and identifies restricted cases where the method works. We checked the preprint argument's stated target and published metadata, not every detail of the final article or all subsequent repair attempts.

For this audit, fixed-field **quadratic** finiteness continues to rest on **Bamón (1986)**, predating and independent of the challenged 1991 proof. Nothing here supplies or refutes a uniform H(2) bound; the four-cycle record and open uniform-finiteness status do not change. [Bamón's primary paper](https://www.numdam.org/item/PMIHES_1986__64__111_0.pdf).

## Search coverage and unresolved items

Searches followed the user-specified arXiv record and subsequent supplied citation leads, Shi's own corrected 1978 claim and 1990 retrospective, the published Yeung finiteness-proof criticism, title/author/citation leads for global H(2)=4/5/6/7 and finiteness, smooth quadratic five/six/seven-cycle claims, 2025–26 classifications and hemicycle results, Gaiko/Qin criticism, and Voronin–Lebedev/Malyarets antecedents. Limited Chinese/Russian phrase searches supplemented English searches. Primary current arXiv version histories were checked. Secondary registries were used as leads, not acceptance evidence.

This is a documented audit, **not an exhaustive bibliography**. Full MathSciNet reviews, a comprehensive zbMATH citation audit, and exhaustive Chinese/Russian literature coverage were unavailable. Specific unresolved source items are:

1. Shi's original 1978 five-cycle preprint (his retrospective and exact attempted family were recovered); Hernandez Rosales's full August 2026 manuscript, its asserted attaining coefficients, and any independent review. No author contact was made.
2. The full 2023 six-cycle antecedent and interior equations of the 2024 economics proceedings. Their status must not be promoted based on the 2026 paper's citations.
3. Exact coefficients and detailed lemmas in Shi's 1988 rebuttal to Qin; the primary abstract was retrieved but not all three pages.
4. Any independent published correction or rebuttal to the 2026 six-cycle article. None was located; the trace calculation here remains explicitly this audit's finding.
5. Full verification of Pedregal's central variational theorem and Gaiko's termination argument. Concrete concerns are recorded; no unlocated review or retraction is asserted.

The surviving explicit six-cycle coefficient family is testable, but the claimed double-Bautin count is invalid. The seven-cycle abstract supplies no reproducible field. Neither justifies adopting a five-or-more-cycle record or abandoning a rigorous construction program.
