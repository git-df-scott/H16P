# Source ledger

The audit prioritizes original papers and author/institution copies. Access
date for links: 2026-09-04.

## Current status and global program

1. A. Gasull and P. Santana, [A note on Hilbert 16th Problem](https://arxiv.org/abs/2407.13465), Proc. Amer. Math. Soc. 153 (2025), 669--677. States that even `H(2)<infinity` is unknown.
2. Y. Villanueva and W. Tucker, [Darboux-type center conditions for families of planar polynomial vector fields](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.22558), arXiv:2602.22558v2 (2026). Current status and `M(2)=3`.
3. R. Bamon, [Quadratic vector fields in the plane have a finite number of limit cycles](https://www.numdam.org/articles/10.1007/BF02699193/), Publ. Math. IHÉS 64 (1986), 111--142.
4. Yu. Ilyashenko, [Centennial history of Hilbert's 16th problem](https://doi.org/10.1090/S0273-0979-02-00946-1), Bull. AMS 39 (2002), 301--354.
5. F. Dumortier, R. Roussarie, C. Rousseau, [Hilbert's 16th problem for quadratic vector fields](https://doi.org/10.1006/jdeq.1994.1061), J. Differential Equations 110 (1994), 86--133.
6. C. Rousseau, [Hilbert's 16-th problem for quadratic vector fields and cyclicity of graphics](https://doi.org/10.1016/S0362-546X(97)00175-2), Nonlinear Analysis 30 (1997). Describes the 121-graphics program.

## Scope exclusions

Results for piecewise-smooth quadratic systems, three-dimensional quadratic
systems, cubic fields, or perturbations by degree greater than two are excluded
from evidence about `H(2)`, except when explicitly labeled as methodological
analogies.
