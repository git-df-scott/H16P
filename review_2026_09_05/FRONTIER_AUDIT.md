## 1. Main finding

There is no missing accepted five-cycle example in the material checked.
There are, however, two consequential coverage gaps:

- Numerical failures and exact results on restricted objects have
  occasionally been promoted into broader route closures. The newest Q4
  endpoint note still does this when it reduces the whole lane to a
  two-alien hypothesis.
- The campaign has not exhausted the established four-cycle seed
  portfolio. The exact GT field, its already recorded conditioning path,
  and published reversible two-center constructions were deprioritized;
  they were not mathematically excluded as sources of different
  finite-amplitude continuations.

The missing construction remains a **common-parameter global
coexistence result**. Separate local cyclicity maxima, a first-order
integral, or a stationary return with nonzero displacement cannot replace
it. A prospective field needs five isolated orbits at the same exact
quadratic coefficients, with complete returns and distinctness verified.

## 3. Specific repair needed in the current Fable endpoint assessment

The numerical itinerary and first-order compatibility work in
[CLAUDE_Q4_ENDPOINT_LANE.md](../CLAUDE_Q4_ENDPOINT_LANE.md) is useful
evidence. It does not establish the final scope claimed in its sections
3–5. The following are review findings, not edits or a reassignment of
Fable's work.

1. The exact triple-center coefficient identity is different from a
   global sign theorem for `X(1;a)`. Positive values sampled over
   `0.05<=a<=0.995` do not prove positivity on the full admissible a
   interval or all limiting regimes. The ensuing universal exclusion of
   that center/endpoint coincidence needs an analytic argument.
2. Counts from 546 sampled two-root points, sampled lobe triples, and
   selected near-center hierarchies do not cover the coefficient region,
   its discriminant strata, or all kappa. They cannot make a universal
   first-order closed count at most three.
3. Theorem N bounds **distinct interior zeros**. It does not prove the
   asserted first-order count at most four after adding a boundary zero,
   nor count multiplicity splittings at the center or endpoint. A boundary
   zero is not itself an isolated periodic orbit.
4. The formula “first-order count plus alien count equals five” needs a
   specified perturbation arc and an actual asymptotic comparison with
   its full return map. It cannot be used as an independent counting law
   before those objects are defined. In particular, two actual endpoint
   cycles need not mean two alien cycles.
5. Consequently “only a two-alien hypothesis remains” is premature.
   Three simple **original** interior Melnikov zeros compatible with two
   original endpoint cycles have not been excluded by these samples.
   Four interior plus one endpoint also remains conditional on the
   presently missing four-zero original integral.

Alien cycles occur in particular unfoldings and are not guaranteed by
the number of saddles or the resonance ratio. GI's quadratic Hamiltonian
example has two actual endpoint cycles with one nearby first-Melnikov
zero, while its closed-annulus theorem separately prevents five there.
This supplies a warning about the counting step, not an existence theorem
for Q4. [Gavrilov–Iliev 2015, Appendix A](https://www.math.univ-toulouse.fr/~gavrilov/publications/50.pdf).

There is a known three-cycle Q4 existence result in Zhao's paper. The
unperformed task is to recover an admissible original-integral direction
and test its **joint** endpoint compatibility, rather than treating the
primitive-H boxes as that direction. [Zhao 2011, Theorem 2](https://arxiv.org/pdf/1011.2253).
No further Q4 sampling or construction was performed in this audit.

## 5. Topology gates for every alternative

Zegeling proves distributions `(n,0)` or `(n,1)`; with four real finite
equilibria the stronger alternatives are `(n,0)` or `(1,1)`.
Exactly five requires `(5,0)` or `(4,1)`. The theorem places no numerical
upper bound on the larger nest.
[Zegeling 2024, Theorems 1.2 and 5.4](https://d-nb.info/1332906729/34).

The objective here is at least five, so the permitted `(5,1)` distribution
would also succeed with six. This distinction matters when an ordinary
fold creates a pair while four existing cycles persist.

The practical consequences are:

- Extra remote cycles while three origin cycles persist would give
  forbidden `(3,2)` or `(3,3)` distributions.
- A multi-cycle two-nest seed cannot retain all its cycles after acquiring
  four real finite equilibria. Creating a finite saddle is not free.
- An ordinary larger-nest fold adds two cycles; `(5,1)` remains permitted
  by this theorem. Six certified cycles would also refute maximum four.
- Original degree must remain at most two after perturbation transport.
  A singular covering need not preserve either the graphic or the class
  of admissible quadratic perturbations.
- Every return bracket must have one continuous full-return itinerary.
  Escape, a long saddle passage, a section pole, or a changed component
  cannot be joined into a displacement sign bracket.

In the current KKL slice, the exact new gate is
`K>K_H(c)=-441J/[125(16-10c)(1+2c)^2]` for negative remote trace.
At positive-K remote Hopf points the local cycle has the wrong stability
for the desired U control. Both are local/equilibrium restrictions;
they do not exclude a finite-amplitude U cycle. The infinity strata at
`c=241/250` and `c=1` require separate charts and itinerary checks.
[Audited derivation](../kkl/notes_other_strata.md).

## 8. What would count as a counterexample

The successful endpoint is one explicit degree-two field and at least
five distinct isolated periodic orbits for that field. A rigorous
analytic existence construction can establish the lower bound; an
explicit rational witness is most reviewable when accompanied by five
disjoint, validated full-return brackets, derivative separation or
interval-Newton inclusion, a topology/nesting account, and independent
replay. A five-zero auxiliary object, five cycles at different
parameters, or a numerical stationary point with nonzero displacement
does not meet that endpoint.

Nothing in this review supplies a candidate or a probability of success.
The practical correction is to preserve the proven exclusions while
keeping untested parameter directions, nonlinear endpoint behavior and
deprioritized certified-four seeds distinct from those exclusions.
